FAQ

What is AGIEV?

AGIEV builds aquarium automation and the supplies that keep your water clean. The controller is still in development.

When can I buy?

Online ordering opens soon. Join the waitlist and you will be the first to know.

What does PPI mean on the filter sponges?

PPI means pores per inch. Lower numbers have larger pores for coarse filtration, and higher numbers have finer pores for polishing.

Which controller is right for me?

AGIEV C1 is for one aquarium with a few modules (12 V). AGIEV C2 is for fishrooms and larger installations, with greater expansion and a wider range of modules (48 V).
