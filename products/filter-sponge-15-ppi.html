Installation tips

  • Rinse the new sponge in dechlorinated water before first use.
  • When cleaning, squeeze it gently in water taken from the aquarium, never under the tap, so the beneficial bacteria stay alive.
  • Cut it to fit your filter chamber, so that water passes through the sponge and not around it.
  • Clean it every few weeks, and replace it when it starts to break down.
